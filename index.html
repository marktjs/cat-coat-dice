🐆 Spotted Modifier (on Mackerel):
💡 X-Linked Non-Orange (X^o) vs. Autosomal Black (B): The X chromosome (ARHGAP36) carries either the wild-type non-orange allele (X^o) or the ~5 kb deletion Orange allele (X^O) (Kaelin et al. 2025; Sasaki et al. 2025). When X^o is active, the autosomal TYRP1 gene (Chr D4) sets the eumelanin shade: Black (B) > Chocolate (b) > Cinnamon (bl).
👁️ Tri-Allelic KIT (W > Ws > w+), FERV1 & Incomplete Penetrance: David et al. (2014, G3) showed that White Spotting (Ws) is a full-length 7,125 bp FERV1 retroviral insertion in KIT intron 1 (Chr B1), whereas Dominant White (W) is a ~620 bp FERV1 solo LTR at the same regulatory site. Unlike many mouse Kit (W) and equine KIT alleles (homozygous lethal/sterile), feline W/W is viable. All W carriers (W/w+, W/Ws, W/W) and Ws/Ws exhibit incomplete penetrance for blue or odd eyes and inner-ear sensorineural deafness, with penetrance highest in W/W.

🧬 Additional Breed-Specific Feline Color Genes Known coat loci outside the 10-locus core dice pool
MC1R (Extension): Recessive e (Amber, Norwegian Forest) and e^r (Russet, Burmese) cause dark eumelanin to fade to amber/red with age.
CORIN (Wide-Band / Sunshine): Recessive variants widen the agouti pheomelanin band, producing Golden Chinchilla and Siberian "Sunshine" coats (distinct from I/I Silver).
TYR Blue-Eyed Albino (c^a) & Mocha (c^m): Additional TYR alleles producing blue-eyed white albinism (c^a/c^a, Imes et al. 2006) and Burmese Mocha.
KIT Gloving (w^g) & Salmiak: Missense KIT variants cause Birman white paws (w^g); a downstream deletion produces the Finnish Salmiak pattern.
ASIP Charcoal (A^Pb): Introgressed Asian leopard cat allele producing the Bengal Charcoal mask and cape when paired with non-agouti a.
