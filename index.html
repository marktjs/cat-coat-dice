💡 Why is "Black" split across X-linked and Autosomal genes? In cats, the X chromosome (O locus, ARHGAP36) carries the switch between Sex-Linked Orange (X^O) and Sex-Linked Black/Dark Eumelanin (X^o). Whenever a cell has an active X^o allele, the autosomal Black / Brown gene (B locus, TYRP1) determines whether that dark eumelanin is Black (B), Chocolate (b), or Cinnamon (b¹)!
